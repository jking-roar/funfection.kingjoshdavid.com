Virus Hunt

Navigate a procedurally generated security grid. Collect glowing viruses, dodge patrolling firewall sentries, and route around walls with the arrow keys.

How to play

  • Use ↑ ↓ ← →, swipe on the board, or tap the on-screen controls to move one tile at a time.
  • Pick up every neon virus to increase your score.
  • Walls block movement. The world layout is generated fresh each run.
  • Red firewall sentries reset you to the start and cost one point if they catch you.
